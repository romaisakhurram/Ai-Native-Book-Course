# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to a journey into the fascinating world of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This book is designed for students, researchers, and engineers who are eager to understand and build intelligent robotic systems that interact with the physical world.

## The Convergence of AI and Robotics

For decades, Artificial Intelligence and Robotics have been distinct but related fields. However, recent advancements in machine learning, perception, and control systems are driving an unprecedented convergence. Physical AI explores how intelligent algorithms can empower robots to perform complex tasks, learn from experience, and adapt to dynamic environments. Humanoid robotics, in particular, stands at the forefront of this revolution, pushing the boundaries of what machines can achieve in human-centric spaces.

## What You Will Learn

Throughout this book, you will:

-   **Understand ROS 2 Fundamentals**: Grasp the core concepts of ROS 2, the leading middleware for robotic development, including nodes, topics, services, and actions.
-   **Master Python for Robotics**: Learn to program robots using `rclpy`, the powerful Python client library for ROS 2.
-   **Model Humanoid Robots**: Discover how to describe complex robot kinematics using URDF (Unified Robot Description Format) and visualize them in simulation environments.
-   **Integrate Advanced AI**: Explore techniques for incorporating AI capabilities, such as perception, navigation, and decision-making, into physical robots.
-   **Build a Capstone Project**: Apply your knowledge to a comprehensive humanoid robot project, bringing together all the concepts learned.

## Who This Book Is For

This book is tailored for:

-   **Beginners in Robotics**: Students and hobbyists with basic programming knowledge (preferably Python) who want to enter the field of robotics.
-   **AI Practitioners**: AI engineers interested in applying their skills to physical systems and understanding the unique challenges of embodied intelligence.
-   **Experienced Roboticists**: Professionals looking to update their knowledge with ROS 2 and modern AI integration techniques.

We believe that by the end of this book, you will have a solid foundation to contribute to the exciting future of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics.
